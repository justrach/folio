# Folio landing-page competitor review

Reviewed **13 September 2026**. Seven products were checked against their own public product pages or documentation. This is a feature and positioning review, not a hands-on performance comparison. Product descriptions below are vendor claims; they do not establish that an advertised intervention causes better rankings, citations, or task completion. Pricing and customer-outcome claims are intentionally excluded.

## Recommendation

Lead with the work a customer can do in Folio: **check a page, inspect the evidence, and review a change**. Show a report that demonstrates that sequence. Keep private agent evaluations and Google Search Console observations visible as separate capabilities.

Monitoring, audits, recommendations, repair exports, and public indexes already appear across this market. Agent-readiness diagnostics and agent-task evaluations also have direct competitors. Folio can emphasize its focused workflow and inspectable records without claiming an exclusive category, superior accuracy, or guaranteed visibility.

The cream-and-green annual-report direction can support this positioning: clear tables, source notes, deliberate type, and one substantial report preview. This is a design recommendation, not evidence of a conversion advantage. Preserve the established serif, logo, and palette; the research does not call for a new brand.

## Folio's current claim boundary

This review uses the current [TODO](../TODO.md), [landing implementation](../src/components/landing.tsx), [evaluation loop](EVALUATION-LOOP.md), and [Search Console documentation](SEARCH-CONSOLE.md) as the local implementation record.

| Can describe in the current pilot | Keep distinct or conditional |
| --- | --- |
| Saved HTML-page audits with a bounded technical rubric and reviewable repair downloads | A technical score does not measure Google position, consumer-AI visibility, or full customer-journey success. |
| Private managed-agent evaluations with retained captures, answers, verifier checks, comparison, and offline verification | A private evaluation is not an independent public cross-company benchmark. Missing reference answers leave facts unmeasured. |
| Explicit read-only Google Search Console imports and private saved reports | These are Google observations for the connected account's accessible properties. They do not establish why a metric changed or feed the technical score. |
| Saved DataForSEO reports and explicitly selected evidence inputs | Estimates, Google observations, and verifier results must keep their separate provenance. |
| An opt-in technical index and illustrative visibility experience | Folio observations require their own capture date, task scope, and status. Illustrative entries remain separate from measured results. |

The implementation-status passages in [the earlier AEO review](research-aeo.md) and [the earlier SEO review](research-seo.md) that call the agent runner unimplemented are now stale. The current managed evaluation workflow exists. Their caution about an independent public benchmark and consumer-AI collectors still applies. This document supersedes those older implementation-status passages; it does not rewrite their historical research record.

## Five exact landing-copy edits

These are recommendations for the landing owner to apply, not changes made by this research task.

| Location | Recommended copy | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero heading | **Make your website easier to understand.** | Names the intended customer outcome in language the page can explain, without implying the product controls answer-engine inclusion. |
| Hero paragraph | **Run a page audit, check an agent's answers against saved evidence, and review changes for your site. Connect Search Console for private Google search reports.** | Explains the implemented work and names Google explicitly. |
| Report-cover title and footer | **A page. Its evidence. The next change.** Footer: **Sample page audit · fixed technical checks.** | Makes the annual-report visual demonstrate the product. Pair it with a single sample finding and repair, replacing the invented cross-company score chart; change the visual and accessible description together. |
| Third “Why Folio” benefit | Heading: **Answers you can check.** Body: **Open an agent's answer beside the saved page and citation checks. See which facts have independent reference answers and which remain unmeasured.** | Describes the live private evaluation path precisely, without calling it an established public benchmark. |
| Final call to action | Heading: **Open a report. Decide what to change.** Body: **Explore the sample workspace, then sign in to work with your own site.** Note: **The pilot includes technical audits and private evaluations. AI visibility charts use illustrative data.** | Gives a concrete next step. Keep any existing access restriction beside the relevant live action rather than implying unrestricted public onboarding. |

Use one primary demo action and a clear sign-in route. Keep methodology and competitor sources available in the existing disclosure rather than making the comparison the main sales story. Do not add unsupported customer logos, made-up growth metrics, comparative superlatives, or “first/only” claims.

## Useful lessons for the index

AgentReady's applicability and coverage distinction is useful for reporting missing evidence. Folio should keep its own measured outcomes, unavailable checks, and task definitions explicit. Its page-readiness rubric does not establish API execution or successful completion of a customer journey.

Each displayed Folio result should answer: which page or interface was tested, with what task and runtime, when it ran, and what was unobserved. Link to Folio's captured evidence and verification details. A public directory listing, a local HTML capture, and a completed sandbox task are three different kinds of evidence. If only the first two exist, the table should say so.

## Research record

All sources in the matrix were read directly. No competitor account was created, no paid evaluation was launched, and no reported competitor score was reproduced. This review makes no cost, conversion, market-size, or performance-superiority claim. Folio's evaluation outcomes are recorded separately from this product research.

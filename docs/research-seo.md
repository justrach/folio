# Folio positioning against SEO suites

Research date: 13 September 2026. Scope: current primary product pages for Semrush, Ahrefs, and Screaming Frog. This is a bounded feature review, not evidence that no other product offers a feature. Folio capabilities below use the implementation scope supplied for this task.

## Finding

“SEO + AEO in one dashboard,” technical checks, historical evidence, suggestions, exported repairs, and public visibility rankings are not individually defensible claims of uniqueness. Folio can credibly emphasize a focused workflow for inspecting page evidence and reviewing changes, while describing its public, reproducible website evaluation ambitions as a roadmap.

## Narrow, defensible positioning

The distinction we can describe now is the product's emphasis: a compact path from a saved page audit to an inspectable finding and a reviewed change that the customer can take back to their site. This is a positioning inference, not a demonstrated superiority claim. There is no customer study here establishing that Folio is faster or easier than the products above.

Folio's potential stronger distinction is a public benchmark of how successfully an agent can complete defined tasks using a website, alongside readiness checks and observed visibility. This must remain a stated direction until collectors, repeatable task suites, actual results, and publication controls are running. Do not collapse those different measurements into a single unexplained score.

## Landing copy safe for the current implementation

**Section label:** WHY FOLIO

**Heading:** A clearer path from finding to fix.

**Intro:** Folio brings page checks, the evidence behind them, and changes you can review into one focused workspace. Open a saved audit, understand what needs attention, and take the next change back to your site.

**Inspect the finding.** See the checks and source details behind your page audit, so you can understand what each result actually measures.

**Keep a record.** Return to saved audits as your site evolves. Each result belongs to the page and capture that produced it.

**Review the change.** Inspect suggested repairs and download the changes you approve for your team to implement.

**A benchmark in the making.** We’re developing the Folio Index: public website evaluations with visible tasks, evidence, and scoring rules. Explore the sample rankings to see the direction.

**FAQ: How is Folio different from an SEO or AEO suite?** Established suites offer broad search research, AI visibility tracking, and technical audits. Folio starts with a focused page-to-change workflow: saved checks, inspectable evidence, and reviewed repair downloads. Live visibility monitoring and measured public rankings are still in development.

Recommended nearby sample label: **Illustrative data — not a measured company ranking.** The leaderboard should preserve that label when shared or exported.

## Current capability versus future claim

| Can describe now | Must remain future or sample |
| --- | --- |
| Deterministic checks on saved page audits | Continuous site-wide crawling or automatic updates |
| Evidence and check details | Verified mentions across live AI providers |
| Reviewed repair downloads | Automatic deployment, guaranteed ranking improvements |
| Sample visibility and leaderboard experience | Actual cross-model evaluations or measured public company ranks |
| Agent API adapter/integration groundwork | Completed collection, evaluation, and durable scheduled orchestration |

## Requirements before publishing measured ranks

Define a versioned task set, category/cohort, model and environment, capture date, allowed tools, success rubric, repeated-run count, and handling of failures or skipped tasks. Link scores to inspectable evidence and show coverage and uncertainty. Allow a company to request correction and rerun. This is a proposed product specification, not a claim about existing Folio functionality.

Keep a readiness score labeled as readiness. A page passing technical checks does not establish that an AI provider retrieved it, cited it, recommended the company, or completed a task successfully.

# Website evaluation and adjacent AI-search competitors

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Scope: a broad website question-evaluation product for different customer segments, including SaaS, ecommerce, services, and developer tools. Brand-visibility and content platforms address related discovery jobs across segments. This is a current first-party product/documentation review, not a hands-on competitor test, procurement quote, exhaustive inventory, or validation of vendor outcome claims. No accounts or paid evaluations were created. An unmentioned capability is unknown, not absent.

## Main finding

Profound, Scrunch, Peec AI, and OtterlyAI serve brand-visibility/content jobs across a mix of customers, and their expanding audit and action workflows overlap with Folio. Neither an inspectable score nor an audit-to-action loop is a defensible exclusivity claim. Folio's own evaluation evidence should establish what its declared task actually measured.

For the present pilot, Folio's strongest pitch is a specific, inspectable customer question: **check whether a reader can recover the facts on a captured website page, inspect the answer and evidence, and review a proposed page change**. Relevant questions vary by segment: plan limits for SaaS, returns and compatibility for ecommerce, eligibility and service coverage for services, or authentication and setup for developer tools. These are proposed task-suite tracks, not claims that specialized suites are implemented. Answer recovery is not proof of completing a purchase, booking, or API integration.

## Adjacent competition

### Profound

**Buyer/workflow.** AEO, content, brand, and agency teams investigate visibility and improve content. Its optimization page describes URL benchmarking against cited competitors, page scoring, structural diagnosis, prioritized changes, briefs/drafts, CMS publishing, and human checkpoints. This directly rules out portraying adjacent competitors as dashboards that never help teams act. Those advertised fixes are not independently proven ranking factors. [Content optimization](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/agents/content-optimization).

**Unit and commercial offer.** Structured prompts run daily across answer engines; tracked responses yield mentions, citations, sentiment, and competitive position. Its price page displays **Starter $99/month**, 50 prompts, ChatGPT, and 100 agent credits; **Growth $399/month**, 100 prompts, three engines, and 400 credits; Enterprise is custom. The page includes a yearly-billing switch and two-month saving language; the extracted view did not establish which toggle state those figures represent, so confirm billing period before quoting an annual total. [Pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing).

**Evidence/overlap.** The public API documentation exposes raw prompt/answer data for custom analysis. Evidence access itself is therefore not unique. Folio's page-bound verifier is a different measurement from observed brand appearances across a prompt panel. [Raw answer examples](https://docs.tryprofound.com/rest-api/examples/answer-engine-insights).

### Scrunch

**Buyer/workflow.** Marketing and technical teams monitor AI presence and website access. Its APIs expose full answers, citations, sentiment, competitors, execution metadata, and agent-traffic logs. These are separate observable layers; retaining source answers is an existing capability. [API explanation](https://scrunch.com/faqs/what-apis-does-scrunch-offer-and-how-do-they-work).

**Action layer.** AXP advertises CDN detection of retrieval bots and delivery of simplified server-rendered HTML, with auditing of access and delivery. Its listed integrations include Cloudflare, Akamai, and Vercel. This is a delivery intervention, materially broader than recommending cleaner page markup. We did not validate the routing, fidelity, or claimed visibility effect. [AXP](https://scrunch.com/platform/agent-experience/).

**Pricing uncertainty.** The fetched `/pricing` representation shows Starter **$300 monthly/$250 monthly billed annually**, 350 custom prompts, and five page audits; Growth is **$500 monthly/$417 billed annually**, with Enterprise custom. [Fetched price page](https://scrunch.com/pricing). Its official FAQ and indexed pricing representation instead show **Core $250/month**, 125 prompts, five audits, and four platforms, with Enterprise custom. This first-party inconsistency is unresolved; do not present either package as a firm comparative quote. [Pricing FAQ](https://scrunch.com/faqs/what-is-the-pricing-for-scrunch-plans).

**Implication.** Folio can sell a manageable documentation review without assuming the buyer wants to introduce a CDN content layer. That is an offer-design choice, not evidence that Scrunch cannot support review-first work.

### Peec AI

**Buyer/unit.** Marketing teams and agencies track brand visibility, position, sentiment, and cited sources. Its documentation describes daily sampling through consumer web interfaces, generally following the logged-out experience; this is a vendor-described collection method, not proof that a prompt panel represents every customer. Different interfaces and model-selection conditions make direct comparisons with Folio's managed reader unsuitable. [Collection methodology](https://docs.peec.ai/intro-to-peec-ai).

**Workflow.** The current plan page describes prompt management, source and competitor analysis, earned/owned opportunities, agent actions, crawler/referral insights, exports, and API/MCP access. Starter, Pro, and Advanced list **50/150/350 prompts**, three selected models, daily collection, and **1/2/5 projects**. The page explicitly calculates monthly answer counts from prompts × active models × frequency, making the sampling budget legible. [Plans](https://peec.ai/pricing).

**Pricing verification limit.** The readable price page did not expose monetary amounts. A separate first-party reference dated July 2026 lists Starter **$95**, Pro **$245**, and Advanced **$495** monthly, but these were not reconfirmed in the current price rendering. Treat them as a dated vendor reference, not verified September checkout prices. [Dated vendor reference](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions).

**Implication.** Source-level opportunities and agent assistance already exist. Folio should distinguish independently supplied reference facts and bounded citation checks from brand-mention/sentiment analytics, while avoiding a claim of superior real-world accuracy.

### OtterlyAI

**Buyer/workflow.** Solo marketers, SMEs, and agencies monitor answers and identify content opportunities. Its product page advertises prompt research, brand/citation tracking, crawlability checks, content auditing and predictions, briefs, and GEO recommendations. Its predictive language is a vendor claim, not established knowledge of why an engine omitted a page. [Product](https://otterly.ai/).

**Unit/methodology.** Brand Coverage is the share of tracked prompts mentioning the brand; its Google AI Overviews denominator excludes cases where an overview did not trigger. Average position counts appearances. The documented “Likelihood to Buy” display is a formula derived from average mention position with a minimum floor, not observed purchases. Folio should not import that label or interpret it as conversion probability. This is a concrete example of why metric definitions and eligible denominators belong beside scores. [KPI definitions](https://help.otterly.ai/brand-report-kpi-definition).

**Public pricing.** The monthly display lists **Lite $29/15 prompts**, **Standard $189/100 prompts**, and **Premium $489/400 prompts**, with daily tracking and four included engines. Claude, Google AI Mode, and Gemini are additional paid coverage. Standard advertises API and MCP access. These prompt subscriptions are not equivalent to Folio's separately authorized paid evaluations. [Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing/).

## Product and landing implications

1. **Lead with customer questions across segments.** Show recognizable website questions about SaaS plans, ecommerce policies, service eligibility, and developer setup. Explain the common evaluation process once, then offer relevant task examples. Specialized suites remain proposed until their tasks, references, assertions, and validation are implemented.
2. **Demonstrate an actual failure-to-review path.** Show one captured page, one question, its returned answer, supporting citation, missing/reference-fact status, and a reviewable change. An illustrative example must say so. If the fix is not linked to a saved retest, describe the operations separately.
3. **Keep four questions distinct.** Can a reader access/extract the page? Does its answer match supplied references? Can an agent execute a real customer task? Does the product appear in sampled consumer-AI answers? Folio's current evidence answers the first two in bounded ways; the latter two need their own execution/collection systems. Purchases, bookings, and integrations each need appropriate task environments.
4. **Build evidence each customer can use.** Preserve task, captured source, runtime/model, suite version, verifier, missing inputs, outcome, and recoverable failure. Keep a shared evidence contract while developing segment-specific questions and success assertions. Add representative execution tests before promising transaction or API/MCP usability coverage. These are recommended investments, not discovered competitor omissions.
5. **Price the job only after validating value.** The published offers mix prompt monitoring, content workflows, and page audits. Credits, prompts, engines, and audit pages differ; dividing prices by prompts produces misleading equivalence. Folio's proposed plans are not active billing, and research alone should not establish pricing or claimed savings.

The earlier [AEO notes](research-aeo.md) contain stale runner-status language. The current [competitor review](COMPETITOR-REVIEW.md) correctly records existing private managed evaluations; consumer-AI collectors and an independent public cross-company execution benchmark remain separate work. Folio's catalog and dated homepage checks are documented in [directory evaluation](DEVELOPER-TOOLS-EVALUATION.md); these checks do not establish API task success.

## Research limitations

Sources were read directly through web retrieval. Some vendor pages expose different or incomplete pricing representations; those uncertainties are retained above. No testimonials, reported lift, accuracy claims, or customer outcomes were treated as independently verified performance.
